# WWE All Stars Recomp 1.6.1

**You need your own copy of the game: a disc image of WWE All Stars (Xbox 360,
USA/Europe) and its Title Update 2, both dumped from your own console. This download
contains no game files, no update and no DLC.**

## What's new

Two new options under **Faster starts** on the launcher's GAMEPLAY tab. Both are off by
default, so the game plays as on the Xbox 360 until you turn them on.

- **Shorter loading screens.** Before every match the game waits about 10 seconds at
  screens where nothing is loading: 2 seconds before the arena intro, and 8 seconds on the
  coaching-tips LOADING screen before the bell. This option cuts those three waits. A 1 vs.
  1 goes from about 75 seconds to about 65 seconds between picking the arena and the bell.
  The tips screen passes too quickly to read. The arena intro and the entrances still play,
  and A still skips them. The option does nothing in online matches, so both players stay
  in step.
- **Skip startup logos.** The game reaches the menu about 12 seconds sooner, without the
  THQ and WWE logo movies. The disclaimer still shows. This one works online too.

## Update

Extract the zip over your existing folder, or let the launcher's update check take you
to this page. Your settings, saves, mods and game files stay where they are.

## Known limits

- Online play pairs only players on the same release: update together with your
  friends.
- Mods are experimental. The format may still change.
- Ranked Match doesn't work yet; Player Match does.
- The in-game store button does nothing; add DLC from the launcher.
- Tested on Windows 11 with an RX 7900 XT, and on a Steam Deck (SteamOS through Proton).
